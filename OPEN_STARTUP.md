# Martie open startup

Martie is built in the open: the code is public, and so are the numbers that
decide whether the project keeps going. This file is where the project
publishes them.

## What is published

| Area          | Figure                                                                | Source of truth                        |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Revenue       | Monthly store proceeds after store fees, per store                    | App Store Connect, Google Play Console |
| Subscriptions | Active premium subscriptions at month end; new and cancelled in month | Store reports, verified purchases      |
| Advertising   | Monthly ad revenue                                                    | Ad network dashboards                  |
| Costs         | Backend, AI, email, developer programs, domains, paid tooling         | Invoices and billing dashboards        |
| Usage         | Monthly active players, quizzes played, D1/D7 retention               | Aggregate Convex and analytics data    |

## Privacy rules

- Only aggregates are published. No figure ever identifies a player, an account
  or a single result.
- Any count below 10 is reported as `<10`.
- Usage figures come from aggregate queries, never from publishing raw rows.

## Cadence

Figures are published once per calendar month, after the stores close the
previous month's reports. Each report states its sources and the date they were
read. Corrections are made in place with a note, never silently.
