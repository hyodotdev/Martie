5 questions, 2 minutes —
a smarter you every day.

Martie is a friendly crow with big round glasses who quizzes you on the things worth knowing. Answer a few quick questions, keep your streak and learn something new every day.

Play on the web No install needed. Purchases are available in the iOS and Android apps.

What you can do

  • 📅

    Daily quiz

    Five fresh questions every day. Finish in about two minutes and keep your streak.

  • 🧠

    20 categories

    Science, world history, law, economy, health, space, food, pop culture and more.

  • 💬

    Ask Martie

    Curious about an answer? Ask Martie and get the story behind it.

  • 🏆

    Leagues

    Climb weekly leagues and the Hall of Fame with friendly competition.

  • 🔁

    Review mistakes

    Missed questions come back later so they stick.

  • ✍️

    Create quizzes

    Submit your own question. When others solve it, you earn feathers.

Available in Korean, English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Vietnamese and Thai.

Built in the open

Martie's source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 license, and the project shares its revenue, costs and aggregate usage. Your account, answers and purchases are never part of it — open code never means open data.
